# Private Equity LBO Model: Mankind Pharma Limited

A professional 5-year Leveraged Buyout (LBO) model evaluating the investment thesis, debt waterfall mechanics, and equity returns for a private equity take-private or buyout of **Mankind Pharma Limited** (NSE: MANKIND).

> **Disclaimer:** These models and reports were created for academic and professional portfolio development purposes. The analysis relies on historical financial disclosures and assumptions constructed solely to demonstrate financial modeling proficiency. They do not constitute formal investment, legal, or financial advice.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project replicates institutional private equity workflows by constructing an entry-to-exit transaction model. It evaluates whether Mankind Pharma's strong cash generation can support a highly leveraged capital structure while hitting targeted internal rates of return (IRR).

## 🛠️ Technical Highlights & Capital Structuring
* **Transaction Sizing:** Entry Enterprise Value of ₹60,408 Cr based on an Entry EBITDA of ₹2,517 Cr at an **Entry Multiple of 24.0x**.
* **Funding Architecture:** Implemented a conservative **5.0x Entry Leverage** structure supported by a sponsor Equity Check of ₹42,168 Cr.
* **Returns & Horizon Analysis:** Formulated comprehensive exit tracking across multiple hold periods (Years 3, 4, and 5) under three operating scenarios:
  * **Base Scenario (5-Year Exit):** Generates an Exit EV of ₹110,806 Cr, resulting in an Equity Proceed of ₹105,092 Cr.
  * **Sponsor Returns:** Achieves a **2.43x MOIC** (Multiple on Invested Capital) and a **19.4% IRR** (p.a.).
  * **Management Incentives:** Modeled a dedicated Management Options hurdle tracking **Mgmt MOIC** (reaching 1.81x by Year 5).
* **Sensitivity Frameworks:** Stress-tested sponsor IRRs across alternative Downside and Upside operating cases, mapping exit multiple contractions and expansions.
